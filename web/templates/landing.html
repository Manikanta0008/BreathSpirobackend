About the App
Understanding respiratory muscle training and how BreathSpiro helps you breathe better
💪

Respiratory Muscle Training

Respiratory muscle training (RMT) strengthens the diaphragm and intercostal muscles through targeted breathing exercises, improving ventilation and reducing breathlessness.

🌬️

Incentive Spirometry

An incentive spirometer encourages sustained maximal inspiration, helping expand the lungs fully and preventing post-surgical or post-illness complications like atelectasis.

📱

Digital Training at Home

BreathSpiro brings professional respiratory training to your phone. Practice guided breathing exercises at home, track your progress, and improve your lung capacity over time.

App Features
Everything you need for effective respiratory muscle training
🧘

Guided Breathing Training

Inhale (4s) → Hold (3s) → Exhale (5s) with visual cues and a circular timer animation

🫁

Lung Expansion Animation

Real-time visual feedback showing lung expansion during each breathing phase

🔄

Breathing Cycle Counter

Track completed cycles with a progress bar. Default: 10 cycles per session

📊

Baseline Assessment

Measure your initial respiratory capacity with breath-hold timing and clinical parameters

⚠️

Safety Alerts

Real-time monitoring with alerts if SpO₂ drops below safe levels during training

📈

Progress Tracking

View your training history, session scores, and performance analysis over time

Clinical Parameters
Comprehensive respiratory health monitoring built into the app
💨

Respiratory Rate

Monitor breaths per minute (normal: 12-20 BPM for adults)

❤️

SpO₂ Monitoring

Record oxygen saturation levels (normal: 95-100%)

📋

Borg Dyspnea Scale (mMRC)

Standardized 0-4 scale measuring breathlessness severity

🚬

Smoking History

Track smoking status and pack-years for risk assessment

💪

MIP & MEP (Muscle Strength)

Maximal Inspiratory/Expiratory Pressure in cmH₂O for strength assessment

⏱️

Training Session Control

Configurable cycles, duration tracking, and breath volume calculation

Safety Disclaimer

BreathSpiro is designed for respiratory muscle training and rehabilitation purposes only. It does not replace professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or clinical spirometry. Always consult your healthcare provider before beginning any respiratory training program, especially if you have existing respiratory conditions. Stop training immediately if you experience dizziness, chest pain, or severe breathlessness.
